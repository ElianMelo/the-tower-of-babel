# Floor and arch support with grounded terrace foliage

The current rule removes the separate square pad when the pillar already connects to an arch directly below it, as requested in the [arch-support reference](../../../Frutiger%20Reference/Asset/Arch-Supported-Pillar-Pads-User.png). **1,505 redundant pads were removed** from the saved tower. The remaining **1,435 support tiles now join sideways into continuous ledges** on shrinking floors, following the [latest reference](../../../Frutiger%20Reference/Asset/Connected-Pillar-Ledge-User.png). They also connect inward to the main deck. The scene still has **86,467 construction assets**; widening the existing supports adds no construction pieces. All 37,560 non-floor assets retain their transforms and stages; the 45-floor layout, stairs, water tiles, pillar meshes and arch meshes are retained. The original 3,441 main-ring extensions remain.

`TowerFloorSupport` queries actual radial sectors, including stair holes. `TryFindInnerConnection` finds an existing inward landing wide enough for the complete support; `TowerFloorLayout.SupportBridge` overlaps that landing. Shrinking-floor bridges stay inward of the incoming outer stair route. `TowerArchSupport` checks a pillar base against the upper beam of an upright saved arch, using its transform, narrow horizontal footprint and an 8 cm contact tolerance. A different radial row, a vertical gap or the arch opening does not count. Bearings are grouped by elevation for authoring queries. Existing progression requires completing the lower floor before construction moves above it.

`TowerGenerator` applies both support options after stair cutting, so regeneration does not reintroduce pads over arches. Foundation or setback pillars without an arch still require deck support. The earlier removal of the extra outer cap row remains in effect. Non-radial custom fixtures retain their existing behavior.

`TowerPillarLedge` shares the angular space between neighbouring pillars, with 4 cm of overlap per edge. At each end of a support run, a tile extends to the neighbouring pillar's existing floor instead of stopping halfway across a remaining gap. The full widened inner edge is sampled against the original mainland, including a 5 cm inward overlap, so a bridge cannot use another support as its only landing. Almost coincident first/last pillars at the 360-degree seam share one layout point; the pillar transforms themselves remain unchanged. The outer support radius stays unchanged, preserving the incoming stair centreline plus a 30 cm player footprint.

`TowerSupportAuthoring.Apply()` recognizes old support sectors and retains only those needed after checking real deck and arch contact. It uses Unity APIs, preserves ordinary deck sectors and remaining construction stages, regenerates foliage against the corrected deck, and saves only this project's saved Tower scene in Edit Mode. Repeated runs use the same rule rather than accumulating duplicate supports.

Bush placement checks the canopy and reserved wind footprint at the centre and 16 perimeter points against real floor sectors. It rejects holes and unsupported ledge edges, keeps wall/stair clearances, and embeds roots by 4 cm. All 164 current shrubs pass the support check and retain the completed-floor visibility gate. One previous placement depended on removed geometry and was omitted by regeneration.

Regression checks include real imported-arch MeshCollider contact, transformed bearings, rejection of gaps and wrong radial rows, MeshCollider raycasts between neighbouring pillars and across the tile joint, overlap across every shrinking-floor bridge's inner edge, continuous ledges between saved pillars, incoming stair clearance, absence of redundant pads on saved and regenerated towers, and every saved pillar and bush across all 45 floors. **19 EditMode checks and 13 final automated PlayMode checks passed.** Runtime checks cover full-tower build/reset, interaction after completing the first floor, cheat controls and tower progression; results are recorded in TowerSupport-PlayMode-Results.json. Implementation and captures used Edit Mode, and final runtime verification restored the saved Tower scene afterward.
